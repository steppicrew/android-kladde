Kladde is a private notebook for your everyday life: recurring tasks and events that remind you, and entries that you tick off or receive from other apps. Everything stays on your phone.

Kladde is young. This version does routines and entries; a diary, your own trackers with charts, export and encrypted sync are planned.

ROUTINES
• Daily, on chosen weekdays, every few days, monthly ("1st and 3rd Monday", "last day of the month") or yearly ("1–12 December")
• With or without a time – tick them off, or just let them happen, like an appointment
• Conditions: "workout, but not on days with choir practice" – if choir is cancelled, the workout is back
• Move or cancel single days, or add an extra one
• Pauses and vacations for all routines or only some, also for groups such as "Work"
• Change a schedule from a date on, without rewriting the past
• Time zones: "6 pm wherever I am" or a fixed "6 pm Berlin" while travelling
• Views: list, week or month, and jump to any date

REMINDERS
• At the time or 5 to 60 minutes before, to the minute if you allow it
• "Done", "Skip" and "In 10 min" right in the notification
• Your own reminder sound

ENTRIES
• Everything you ticked off, with its time
• Values from other apps, e.g. drinks from "Steppi's Beer Counter" – only from apps you allow one by one, and they can only write, never read

PRIVATE
• Kladde has no internet permission: your data can't leave your phone
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account
• No Android cloud backup
• Privacy policy: https://steppicrew.github.io/android-kladde/
